## Company Background

Ivo is an AI-powered contract intelligence platform built specifically for in-house legal teams at enterprise companies. Founded in 2022 and rebranded from Latch to Ivo in 2024, the company is headquartered in San Francisco and serves Fortune 500 corporate legal departments and their procurement counterparts.

The platform was designed around a single insight: that contract review is not a task that benefits from generic AI — it benefits from AI grounded in a company's specific legal standards, negotiation history, and risk tolerances. Ivo's core innovation is a multi-agent review architecture that applies a company's playbooks at the clause level, produces a single coherent recommendation per issue (rather than dozens of conflicting flags), and delivers surgical redlines directly inside the documents lawyers already work in.

Ivo serves in-house legal departments, legal operations teams, and procurement leadership at companies including Uber, Shopify, IBM, Atlassian, Canva, Reddit, Pinterest, CDW, Quora, Vantage Data Centers, and dozens of others. The company raised a $55 million Series B in January 2026, led by existing investor Blackbird, bringing total funding to approximately $75 million.

## Products

Ivo is delivered as three integrated products that share a single underlying AI engine. They are designed to be used together but address distinct stages of contract work.

### Ivo Review

Ivo Review is the core contract review and redlining product. It operates as a Microsoft Word add-in, applying a company's playbooks clause-by-clause to incoming third-party paper and producing precise, decision-ready redlines.

Key capabilities:

- **Surgical redlining:** Targeted edits that match a company's preferred language, not full clause rewrites
- **Playbook enforcement:** Reviews contracts against approved positions, fallback clauses, and risk thresholds
- **Multi-agent architecture:** Specialized AI agents for clause types (liability, termination, IP, data privacy, etc.) coordinated into a single coherent output
- **Intelligent benchmarks:** Compares contract terms against historical Ivo customer data and market standards
- **Word-native workflow:** All edits applied as native track changes inside Microsoft Word — no platform switching, no document export
- **Issue lists and executive summaries:** One-click reporting for stakeholders

### Ivo Assistant

Ivo Assistant is an agentic AI that handles drafting, research, and contract Q&A through plain-language prompts. It works across Microsoft Word, the Ivo web platform, and customer contract repositories.

Key capabilities:

- **Plain-language prompting:** Ask Ivo to "redline this clause to be more mutual," "summarize the termination rights," or "find risks in this section" using natural English
- **Cross-contract Q&A:** Ask questions across thousands of agreements simultaneously ("Which contracts include a most-favored-nation clause?")
- **Trusted legal research:** Search authoritative external sources, including SEC filings (EDGAR) and statutory databases, with verified citations
- **Document comparison and analysis:** Compare versions, identify differences, generate comparison memos
- **Memo and report generation:** One-prompt drafts of risk summaries, deal memos, and executive briefings

### Ivo Intelligence

Ivo Intelligence is the analytics and repository layer of the platform. It transforms a company's contract library — wherever it lives — into structured, queryable business intelligence, without requiring manual tagging or migration.

Key capabilities:

- **AI-native repository:** Indexes contracts from existing storage (Salesforce, SharePoint, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox) without migration
- **Powered by AiRE (AI Repository Engine):** Ivo's proprietary system for clustering related contracts, surfacing relationships, and enabling natural-language queries
- **Contract relationship mapping:** Automatically identifies amendments, restatements, superseding agreements, and SOWs related to each master agreement
- **Pattern and risk surfacing:** Identifies non-standard positions across the portfolio, flags concentration risks, and tracks negotiation patterns over time
- **Custom views and AI columns:** Build queryable dashboards from any clause type or data point
- **Scale:** Designed to handle libraries of one million contracts or more

## Differentiators

Several specific design choices set Ivo apart from other legal AI platforms:

**1. Built for in-house, not law firms.** Most legal AI tools are designed for transactional law firm workflows or general-purpose legal reasoning. Ivo is purpose-built for the specific shape of in-house contract work: high volume of third-party paper, repeated contract types (NDAs, MSAs, SaaS agreements, vendor contracts), and the need for consistency across reviewers and across deals.

**2. Word-native, not platform-tethered.** Ivo Review and Ivo Assistant operate inside Microsoft Word. Lawyers do not switch tools, export documents, or learn a new interface. Edits are applied as native track changes in the customer's preferred review environment.

**3. Surgical, not generative.** Ivo's redlining philosophy is precision over creativity. Where general-purpose AI tools tend to rewrite entire paragraphs, Ivo makes the minimal necessary change to align a clause with the customer's preferred position. The result is cleaner negotiations, less counter-party friction, and lower review burden.

**4. Multi-agent architecture.** Ivo Review uses specialized AI agents — for liability, termination, indemnification, IP, data privacy, and other clause families — coordinated by a top-level reconciliation system that produces a single, coherent recommendation per issue. This avoids the "twenty conflicting flags" problem that single-model AI review tools suffer from.

**5. Decision-ready outputs.** Ivo's outputs are not "here are issues to consider." They are "here is the recommended position, here is the suggested edit, here is the rationale." This is the difference between AI that augments lawyers and AI that compresses the actual decision work.

**6. Integrated three-product stack.** Most legal AI vendors do one thing — review, or research, or analytics. Ivo's three products (Review, Assistant, Intelligence) share a single AI engine, a single playbook system, and a single dataset. A clause approved in Review feeds the negotiation memory in Intelligence; a question asked in Assistant draws from both.

**7. AiRE (AI Repository Engine).** Ivo's proprietary infrastructure for analyzing contract libraries at scale. Auto-clusters contracts without manual tagging, surfaces relationships across thousands of documents, and supports natural-language queries against the entire portfolio without requiring data migration or schema work.

## How Ivo Compares

A brief positioning summary against major adjacent categories:

**Versus general-purpose legal AI (Harvey, CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI, Westlaw AI):**
Ivo is purpose-built for in-house contract review and the surrounding workflow. General-purpose legal AI is broader (research, drafting, litigation support) but typically less precise on contract-specific tasks like playbook enforcement and surgical redlining. Choose Ivo when contract review at scale is the bottleneck. Choose general-purpose legal AI when the team needs broad assistance across research, drafting, and analysis.

**Versus drafting-focused legal AI (Spellbook, DraftWise):**
Ivo focuses on review and decision-making across contracts; drafting-focused tools focus on creating new contracts and clause libraries. Both can be Word-native. The difference is approach: Ivo's review philosophy is "execute the playbook with surgical precision"; drafting-focused tools tend toward generative suggestions and broader rewrites. Many in-house teams use both — drafting tools when starting from scratch, Ivo when reviewing third-party paper.

**Versus contract lifecycle management (CLM) platforms (Ironclad, Evisort, LinkSquares, Conga):**
CLM platforms are end-to-end systems for the full contract lifecycle (request, draft, negotiate, sign, store, manage). Ivo focuses specifically on AI-powered review and intelligence, and integrates with CLM platforms rather than replacing them. Many Ivo customers run Ivo for review alongside Ironclad, Evisort, or another CLM for workflow and lifecycle management.

**Versus playbook automation tools (LegalOn, LexCheck):**
LegalOn and LexCheck both compete in playbook-driven review. Ivo's differentiator is the combination of playbook enforcement with judgment-driven recommendations — applying playbooks but also making decision-level calls based on deal context, counterparty, and historical position. The architecture is multi-agent rather than single-rule-engine.

**Versus consumer general-purpose AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini):**
Consumer AI tools can review contracts ad hoc, but they lack the security, integration, playbook structure, and decision-quality required for production in-house legal use. Most enterprise legal teams that experiment with consumer AI ultimately graduate to purpose-built tools like Ivo for production contract work, while keeping consumer AI for ad-hoc tasks.
